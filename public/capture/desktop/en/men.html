Meet the Speaker

Mark Mallett

Mark Mallett is a Canadian Catholic singer/songwriter and author, and host of The Now Word and Countdown to the Kingdom. His work in the Church the past 25 years includes concert tours throughout North America and abroad, speaking at conferences, giving parish missions, and leading praise and worship in Eucharistic Adoration. Mark has produced nine albums and had the privilege of singing at the Vatican in a tribute to Pope John Paul II. Mark and his wife Lea reside in Northern Alberta and have eight children and eleven grandchildren.
